Privacy
Local by design
Tracker entries are stored in this browser using localStorage. Hosting the app does not place your personal entries in the public source repository. If you clear site data or change devices without a backup, local entries may be lost.

  
Evidence framework
What the app is built around
Behavioral support: evidence-based tobacco cessation guidance includes behavioral support; digital tools can be used as adjuncts or self-management tools.
Medical options: approved cessation treatments can be useful depending on the person and context. The app does not recommend a personal medication regimen.
Relapse prevention: coping strategies and relapse prevention should be discussed early and revisited over time.
Sources: WHO Clinical treatment guideline for tobacco cessation in adults (2024); NICE NG209, Tobacco: preventing uptake, promoting quitting and treating dependence.
